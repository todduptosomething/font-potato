Turn your handwriting into a real, installable font

Print a sheet. Scribble on it like nobody's watching. Snap a photo. Font Potato mashes every letter and hands you back a font for anywhere fonts go — documents, designs, websites, ransom notes, anything.

  • All 94 keyboard characters, because nobody wants a font that chokes on a semicolon
  • Alternate characters so repeated letters actually look like handwriting, not a stamp
  • Fine-tune your font with multiple adjustment options

Made for the terminally lazy typographer.

The template

Print it, write one letter per box with a dark pen, then photograph the whole sheet — flat, evenly lit, all four corner marks visible. The marks let the app read every letter precisely.

Template Tip: Use the baseline to size and place your letters consistently. Don't draw outside the box or your letter won't be scanned completely.
